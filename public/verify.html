Verify a certificate

Enter the certificate number printed on a Security Training Academy Certificate of Completion, or scan its QR code, to confirm the recipient, the course and the completion date.

Certificate number

Certificate numbers look like SEC-ACADEMY-2026-000001 and are printed in the lower left of the certificate.

✓

Certificate verified

Scanning a certificate? The QR code opens this page with the result already loaded — there is nothing to type.

What this page shows. A certificate is only displayed when a specific certificate number is supplied. The academy does not publish a list of issued certificates, and this page cannot be used to browse them.

Not what you expected? If any detail shown here does not match the printed document in front of you, do not rely on that document.
