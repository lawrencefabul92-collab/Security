About

Security Training Academy

Security Training Academy is the professional training programme of the Philippine Security and Safety Professional. It exists for one purpose: to give people already working in security the structured knowledge that experience alone does not supply.

Courses are delivered online and self-paced so that they fit around shift work, and they are assessed on written work drawn from the learner's own site. On completion, an official Certificate of Completion is issued under the authority of the academy's authorised signatory and can be verified publicly at any time.

What the certificate is. A Certificate of Completion records that a person completed a course of study with this academy. It is not a government licence, a professional accreditation, or a substitute for any credential required by law or by a regulator.
